## B. Candidate LIS Variables Identified
- **SMC** (SoilMoist_tavg): Multilayer soil moisture (often 4 layers).
- **SH2O** (SoilMoist_tavg equivalent for liquid): Liquid soil moisture.
- **ET / Qle**: Look for `Evap_tavg` or `Qle_tavg`. If `Qle_tavg` (latent heat) is present but not ET, ET can be derived via latent heat of vaporization.
- **Runoff**: `Qs_tavg` (surface) and `Qsb_tavg` (baseflow).
- **Precipitation**: `Rainf_tavg` (if present in LIS_HIST, serves as the effective forcing).
- **Water Table**: `ZWT_tavg` or `WaterTableD_tavg`.

## C. RZSM Calculation Rule
RZSM will be computed as a weighted average of SMC layers 1–3:
```text
RZSM = (SMC1*0.1 + SMC2*0.3 + SMC3*0.6) / 1.0
```
This represents the 0–1 m root-zone soil moisture. Individual soil layers will be kept as optional predictors.
